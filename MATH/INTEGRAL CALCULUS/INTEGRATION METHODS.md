## Chebyshev's theorem for binomial integrals
$$\int x^m(a+bx^n)^p\,\mathrm dx$$
If at least one of the three cases are met, then the integral has elementary function solution
### Cases
- **Case 1:** $p\in\mathbb Z$ 
- **Case 2:** $\frac{m+1}{n}\in\mathbb Z$ 
- **Case 3:** $\frac{m+1}{n}+p\in\mathbb Z$
