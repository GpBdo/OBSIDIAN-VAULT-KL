The dimension of a finite-dimensional vector space $(\dim V)$ is the length of any basis of the vector space $(V)$. Let the vectorial space $\mathbb F^n$ then: $\mathrm{dim}(\mathbb F^n)=n$
**Consequences**
- Any basis of a finite-dimensional vectorial space has the same length.
- Any linearly independent vectors with length $\dim V$ is a basis of the finite-dimensional vectorial space $V$.
- Any list of vectors that spans a vectorial space $V$ with length $\dim V$ is a basis of $V$.
# Dimension of a subspace
Let a subspace $U$ of a finite-dimensional vectorial space $V$, then: $\dim U\leq\dim V$. Where if $\dim U=\dim V$, then $U=V$.
# Dimension of a sum of vectorial spaces
Let $V$ and $U$ subspaces of a common finite-dimensional vectorial space, then:
$$\dim(V+U)=\dim V+\dim U-\dim(V\cap U)$$
