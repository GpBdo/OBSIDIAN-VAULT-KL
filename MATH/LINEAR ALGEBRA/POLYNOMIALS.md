# Polynomial
The function $p:\mathbb F\to\mathbb F$ is a polynomial with coefficients in $\mathbb F$ if there exists $a_i$ such that:
$$p(z)=a_0 +a_1z + a_2 z^2 +\ldots+ a_mz^m$$
And $\mathbb F[z]$ is a set of all polynomials over $\mathbb F$:
$$\mathbb F[z]:=\lbrace p\in\mathbb F^\mathbb F\mid p \text{ is a polynomial}\rbrace$$
## Degree of a polynomial
Let the polynomial $p:\mathbb F\to\mathbb F$ with coefficients $a_i\in\mathbb F$ and $a_m\ne0$:
$$p(z)=a_0+a_1z+a_2z^2+\ldots+a_mz^m$$
The degree of the polynomial $p$ is $m$: $\mathrm{deg}(p)=m$ 
The dimension of the polynomial $p$ is $m+1$: $\mathrm{dim}(p)=m+1$

### $\mathbb F_m[z]$
Is the set of all polynomial with coefficients in $\mathbb F$ and degree at most $m$:
$$\mathbb F_m[z]=\mathrm{span}(1,z,z^2,\ldots,z^m)$$
