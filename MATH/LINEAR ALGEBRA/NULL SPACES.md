# Null space
Let V and W finite-dimensional vectorial space and let a linear map $T\in\mathcal L(V,W)$, then the null space of  the linear map $T$ is:
$$\text{null }T=\lbrace v\in V\mid Tv=0\rbrace$$
**Consequences:**
- The null space of a linear map $\in\mathcal(V,W)$ is a subspace of  $V$.
## Injectivity
Let a linear map $T\in\mathcal L(V,W)$, then:
$$T\text{ is injective}\iff\text{null }T=\lbrace0\rbrace$$
---
# Fundamental theorem of linear maps
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space and $T\in\mathcal L(V,W)$, then the range of T is finite dimensional and:
$$\dim V=\dim \text{null }T+\dim\text{range }T$$
