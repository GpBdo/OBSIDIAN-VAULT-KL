Let $F$ be a function $F:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^3$, then the curl of the vector field is:
$$\mathrm{curl}\,(\vec F)=\nabla\times\vec F$$
where the integral definition of curl is:
$$\mathrm{curl}\,(\vec F)\cdot\hat n=\lim_{\Delta A\to0}\frac{1}{\Delta A}\oint_{\partial A}\vec F\cdot\mathrm d\vec r$$
The unitary vector $\hat n$ is the direction of the area vector $\Delta A$.
**Interpretation:**
The curl of a vector field at a point $a$ is the vector pointing in the direction of the axis of net rotation of the vectors near the point $a$. 
- $|\mathrm{curl}(\vec F)|=2\omega$
