Let $f$ be a function $f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R$, then the laplacian of $f$ is:
$$\nabla\cdot(\nabla f)=\nabla^2f=\sum^n_{i=1}\frac{\partial^2f}{\partial^2x_i}$$
**Interpretation:**
The laplacian at a point $a$ indicates the (signed) difference between the value of $f$ at $a$ and the average value of $f$ at nearby poins.
