Let $F$ be a function $F:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^n$, then the divergence of the function $f$ is:
$$\mathrm{div}(\vec F)=\nabla\cdot\vec F=\sum^n_{i=1}\frac{\partial F_i}{\partial x_i}=\lim_{\Delta V\to0}\frac{1}{\Delta V}\iint_{\partial V}\vec F\cdot\mathrm d\vec S$$
**Interpretation**
It is the rate of trend of dispersion (Divergence) or concentration (Convergence) of vectors in a vector field. 
- The double integral indicates the total contribution of the vector $\vec F$ at every point in the surface $(\partial V)$ in the direction of $\mathrm d\vec S$. We divide by $\Delta V$, because we change the extensive property (Total flux) into a extensive property.
