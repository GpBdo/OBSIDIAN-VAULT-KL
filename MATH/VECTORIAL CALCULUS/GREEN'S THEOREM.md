Let $F$ be a function $F:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$, let $D$ a region defined on $F$, then:
$$\oint_{\partial D}\vec F\cdot\mathrm d\vec r=\iint_D(\nabla\times\vec F)\cdot\mathrm d\vec S$$
**Interpretation:**
The circulation around the boundary $(\partial D)$ of the region $D$ equals the sum of the rotations of the points in the region $D$.
